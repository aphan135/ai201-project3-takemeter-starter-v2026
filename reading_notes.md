# Reading notes — before labelling anything

## Community

**Picked:** r/movies

**Why it works:** It is a completely public, high-volume forum with millions of active users generating thousands of text posts daily. It features a wide variation in post quality—ranging from single-sentence casual opinions to multi-paragraph analytical essays with box office stats and director quotes. It covers the entire landscape of film rather than being built around a single franchise, TV show, or recent release, preventing spoiler-heavy or hyper-specific niche jargon.

**Can I get 200 public posts from it?** Yes, by retrieving recent text submissions (discussion posts, recommendation requests, scene breakdowns, and news reviews) directly from the public r/movies feed without needing authentication or private access.
